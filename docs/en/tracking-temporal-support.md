## Intervention decision and next gate

**Decision: retain the current reference; do not add a temporal veto, relax equivalence or select N consecutive frames.** Geometry often persists while the signed-gap condition does not, and most prefix interruptions are missing accepted observations, not observed negative geometry. Structural groups alone and retained predictions cannot identify which physical association is correct.

The next candidate mechanism is **past-only accepted-observation support qualification plus update-displacement protection**: any proposed rule must distinguish an actively observed competitor from a retained prediction, use only keys/states available at the decision time, and keep occupied/same-update, occupied/replacement, occupied/loss and available/replacement risks separate. Unknown comparison is insufficient evidence for either a positive or negative identity decision. The +1…+30 reference observations in this report are diagnostic outcomes and must never become features of an online decision at the anchor.

Before implementing a rule, separately freeze its causal feature definition, handling of missing/censored histories, eligibility and full-scene FN/FP/IDSW/IDF1/HOTA harm criteria, with genuinely new confirmation sources. Any uncertainty/perturbation model needs independent justification. A duration, tolerance or risk ranking must not be fitted to these 197 outcomes; preserve weak inputs and expose failures, including lost long-occlusion support and the bus issue. **Definitions and descriptive analysis complete; physical identity validity, causal intervention benefit and independent reliability acceptance remain open.**
