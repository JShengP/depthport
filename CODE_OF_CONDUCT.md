# Community conduct / 社群行為準則

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique code and evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or limit participation to protect contributors.

請尊重他人、具體且建設性地討論，評論程式與證據而非攻擊個人。禁止騷擾、威脅、歧視辱罵、肉搜及公開他人隱私；維護者可刪除內容或限制參與。

Ask maintainers for a private reporting channel without posting private details publicly. This snapshot does not invent a maintainer address or promise 24/7 moderation. English and Traditional Chinese contributions are equally welcome.

需要通報時先詢問私人管道，不公開隱私；本版不虛構維護者聯絡方式，也不承諾全天候管理。英文與繁體中文貢獻皆歡迎。
